## How do I create my first project?

**Also asked as:** how do I start a new project, where's the new project button

**Needs:** You should be a user for project access attached on your role.
No plan restriction.

1. On the left side navigation of the application find **Projects** and clicked.
2. Click on the **New** button at the top right.
3. Enter a **Project name**.
4. Scroll down to the bottom and hit **Save** button.

You land on project detail view. The project is live immediately; there's
no publish step.

**Gotcha:** The system allow duplicate names just be mind full of it.
